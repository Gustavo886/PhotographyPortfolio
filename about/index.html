PLATE 01 • FLASH & SHADOW
01 / PHILOSOPHY

THE RAW GAZE

I'm a documentary and street photographer based between Mexico City and global transit points. My work documents the tension, intimacy, and unvarnished rituals of life, moving between the grain and mood of film and the clarity of a clean, detailed frame.

The camera is an instrument of direct engagement, not distant observation. Photographs are made close to the subject and treated as reporting rather than decoration: what is in the frame has to be true to what happened in front of the lens.

PLATE 02 • ARENA MÉXICO
02 / METHOD

SHARP, DIZZY, OR SOMEWHERE BETWEEN

There is no single look, only the one the moment asks for. Sometimes that is film logic: grain, simulation, or emulsion. Sometimes it is clean, high-detail files where every texture holds. And sometimes it is deliberate blur, a dizzy frame that records motion and feeling rather than facts.

Gear stays minimal: camera and lens. A limited kit forces decisions about distance, timing, and light to happen at capture, not in post.

PLATE 03 • SHINJUKU NIGHTS
03 / NARRATIVE

THE UNCUT SEQUENCE

The full sequence is the unedited diary of the photographer. It exposes the near-misses, sudden confrontations, and the single fraction of a second when intuition aligns with reality.

Looking across a whole shoot reveals the process behind the single frame: the shifts in distance, geometry, and light until the decisive instant locks in.
