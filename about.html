PLATE 01 • FLASH & SHADOW
01 / PHILOSOPHY

THE RAW GAZE

Gustavo Martinez is a documentary and street photographer based between Mexico City and global transit points. Working predominantly with high-contrast monochrome and pushed film emulsions, his work documents the tension, intimacy, and unvarnished rituals of urban life.

Rejecting detached telephoto observation, the camera is an instrument of direct engagement. Photographs are made within arm's reach—embracing the friction, kinetic velocity, and raw vulnerability of the subject.

PLATE 02 • ARENA MÉXICO
02 / METHOD

35MM & THE DARKROOM

Every project originates on physical 35mm film rolls—Kodak Tri-X pushed in D-76, Ilford HP5+ in Microphen, and high-grain T-Max 3200. The mechanical discipline of manual rangefinders forces deliberate physical positioning and decisive timing.

Working with fixed 28mm and 35mm lenses eliminates the illusion of detachment. Each negative captures the sweat, flash strobe falloff, and unscripted spontaneity of the street in its most visceral form.

PLATE 03 • SHINJUKU NIGHTS
03 / NARRATIVE

THE UNCUT SEQUENCE

The contact sheet is the uncut diary of the photographer. It exposes the sequence of near-misses, sudden confrontations, and the singular fraction of a second when intuition aligns with reality.

Looking across a full roll reveals the living process behind the single frame—the physical shifts in distance, geometry, and light until the decisive instant locks into silver.
